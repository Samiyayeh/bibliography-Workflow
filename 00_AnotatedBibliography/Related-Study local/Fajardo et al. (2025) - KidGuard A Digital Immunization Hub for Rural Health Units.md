# KidGuard: A Digital Immunization Hub for Rural Health Units

**Category:** [[Related Study local]]

**Ma. Rica Mae R. Fajardo, Jane Marie M. Matalote, Chelsie N. Quizon, Dionalyn Mae G. Galang, Jim M. Gonzales, and Jodell R. Bulaclac. 2025. KidGuard: A Digital Immunization Hub for Rural Health Units. *International Journal of Innovative Science and Research Technology* 10, 9 (2025), 1912–1921. https://doi.org/10.38124/ijisrt/25sep1351**

- **Summary:** To overcome systemic record-keeping delays and fragmented patient tracking in Philippine rural health facilities, this study developed and assessed a digital health management hub using the Agile Systems Development Life Cycle, demonstrating significant operational acceleration in record retrieval and obtaining composite ISO/IEC 25010 quality scores of 4.7 in functionality and 4.6 in usability.
- **Relevance:** The empirical evaluation validates the technical architecture of the proposed Naga City Health Office system, as the high ISO/IEC 25010 scores in functionality (4.7) and usability (4.6) confirm that replacing paper-based registries with a centralized, browser-accessible database streamlines intake processing and prevents medical record fragmentation in municipal public health settings.
- **Source Reference(s):**
  - Abstract & Section 4 (Results and Evaluation), pp. 1912–1921.
  - Direct Quote: *"Results indicate substantial improvements compared to the manual system, with highest gains in report generation speed (+3.5), transferability of records (+3.2), and communication of schedules (+2.6). Overall, KidGuard scored within the Agree to Strongly Agree range across all eight quality dimensions, with functionality (4.7) and usability (4.6) receiving the highest ratings."* (Abstract, p. 1912)
