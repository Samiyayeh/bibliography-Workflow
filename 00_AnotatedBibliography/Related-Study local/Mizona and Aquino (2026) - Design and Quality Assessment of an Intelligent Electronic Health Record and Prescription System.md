# Design and Quality Assessment of an Intelligent Electronic Health Record and Prescription System for Low-Resource Clinic Settings

**Category:** [[Related Study local]]

**Isaiah Joshua R. Mizona and Jet C. Aquino. 2026. Design and Quality Assessment of an Intelligent Electronic Health Record and Prescription System for Low-Resource Clinic Settings. *International Journal of Advanced Multidisciplinary Research and Studies* 6, 1 (2026), 3035–3052. https://doi.org/10.62225/2583049X.2026.6.1.5884**

- **Summary:** To address fragmented clinical documentation and administrative inefficiencies in Philippine municipal health units, this developmental study engineered and assessed a centralized electronic health record and prescription platform using the Agile software development lifecycle, demonstrating substantial improvements in record retrieval speed and verified compliance across all eight ISO/IEC 25010 software product quality dimensions.
- **Relevance:** The empirical evaluation substantiates the centralized database architecture of the proposed Naga City Health Office system, as the verified ISO/IEC 25010 ratings for performance efficiency and functional adequacy confirm that replacing paper-based records with an integrated digital repository accelerates patient data retrieval and eliminates chart fragmentation during clinical consultations.
- **Source Reference(s):**
  - Abstract & Article Body (pp. 3035–3052).
  - Direct Quote: *"The outcomes indicated that IT experts assessed the system as highly performant, efficient, reliable and secure in terms of all ISO/IEC 25010 factors. The acceptability for the end users was also high as reflected by faster patient record retrieval, improved workflow, shorter time to script, increased prescription accuracy and usability."* (Abstract, p. 3035)
