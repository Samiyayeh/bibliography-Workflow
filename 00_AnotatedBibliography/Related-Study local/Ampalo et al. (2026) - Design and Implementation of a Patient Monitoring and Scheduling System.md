# Design and Implementation of a Patient Monitoring and Scheduling System for Healthcare Institutions

**Category:** [[Related Study local]]

**ACM Citation:**
Ampalo, C., Dangasi, S. K., and Galvez, A. 2026. Design and Implementation of a Patient Monitoring and Scheduling System for Healthcare Institutions. *International Journal on Science and Technology* 17, 3 (2026), 11370. https://doi.org/10.71097/IJSAT.v17.i3.11370

- **Summary:**  
  Ampalo et al. (2026) developed and evaluated a web-based patient monitoring and scheduling system for the Rural Health Unit of Bayog, Zamboanga del Sur using the Agile software development lifecycle, demonstrating that automating patient intake workflows and record retrieval achieved high compliance across ISO/IEC 25010 software quality characteristics.

- **Relevance:**  
  The study provides localized empirical data on digitizing primary care operations within a Philippine municipal health unit, evaluating software quality through direct assessment by healthcare personnel. Published in the *International Journal on Science and Technology*, the research verifies that implementing structured digital scheduling reduces administrative burdens associated with manual paper records. However, the evaluation focuses on a single rural facility, which may require scaling adjustments when applied to higher-density urban health offices. These findings justify the methodological framework of the proposed Naga City Health Office project by validating Agile development and the ISO/IEC 25010 quality model in a local public health setting. Specifically, the authors' verification of usability and efficiency metrics supports the proposed system's module integration, ensuring that automated queue scheduling aligns with frontline clinical workflows in Philippine local government units.
