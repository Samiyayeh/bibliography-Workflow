# Optimizing Vaccine Access: A Web-Based Scheduling System with Geo-Tagging Integration and Decision Support for Local Health Centers

**Category:** [[Related Study local]]

**Jayson Angelo Batoon and Keno Cruz Piad. 2023. Optimizing Vaccine Access: A Web-Based Scheduling System with Geo-Tagging Integration and Decision Support for Local Health Centers. *Open Journal of Applied Sciences* 13, 5 (2023), 720–730. https://doi.org/10.4236/ojapps.2023.135057**

- **Summary:** To improve patient appointment management and resolve administrative bottlenecks in Philippine municipal health clinics, this study developed a web-based local health center scheduling platform using Agile Scrum methodology, achieving an overall composite score of 4.62 across the ISO/IEC 25010 software quality characteristics during stakeholder evaluation.
- **Relevance:** The empirical evaluation reinforces the methodological framework of the proposed Naga City Health Office system, as the 4.62 ISO/IEC 25010 quality rating indicates that replacing manual intake with centralized, browser-based scheduling in Philippine local health units meets strict usability and performance efficiency standards for frontline healthcare workers.
- **Source Reference(s):**
  - Abstract & Section 4 (Results and Discussion), pp. 720–730.
  - Direct Quote: *"Following the assessment, the system was given an average total weighted mean score of 4.62, which represents a verbal interpretation of 'strongly agree'. This score demonstrates that the evaluators were in agreement that the system met the requirements of ISO 25010 for Product Software Quality Standards."* (p. 720)
