# Development and Evaluation of the COVID-19 Vaccination Management System for the Local Government Units

**Category:** [[Related Study local]]

**Jon Laurence B. Wenceslao. 2022. Development and Evaluation of the COVID-19 Vaccination Management System for the Local Government Units. *The Seybold Report* 17, 06 (2022), 297–308. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.6641053**

- **Summary:** To eliminate manual bottlenecks and fragmented record-keeping in municipal health delivery, this developmental study engineered and assessed a web-based patient management and notification system for the Rural Health Unit of Estancia, Iloilo using Rapid Application Development, with 47 stakeholders confirming high operational usability and software quality compliance under the ISO/IEC 25010:2011 model.
- **Relevance:** The empirical evaluation substantiates the module routing and automated notification architecture of the proposed Naga City Health Office system, as the verified ISO/IEC 25010 ratings from public health personnel demonstrate that replacing manual clerical logs with a web-accessible management system optimizes administrative workflow and ensures consistent patient tracking across local government health units.
- **Source Reference(s):**
  - Abstract & Main Text, pp. 297–308.
  - Direct Quote: *"Findings showed that the levels of usability, as perceived by both residents and health personnel, was described as very good. On the other hands, the degree of quality of the system prototype was viewed by the expert evaluators as good."* (Abstract, p. 297)
