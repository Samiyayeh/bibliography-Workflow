# Exploring the Landscape of Health Information Systems in the Philippines: A Methodical Analysis of Features and Challenges

**Category:** [[Related Study local]]

**ACM Citation:**
Tinam-isan, M. A. C. and Naga, J. F. 2024. Exploring the Landscape of Health Information Systems in the Philippines: A Methodical Analysis of Features and Challenges. *International Journal of Computing and Digital Systems* 15, 1 (2024), 225–237. https://doi.org/10.12785/ijcds/160118

- **Summary:**  
  Tinam-isan and Naga (2024) conducted a methodical analysis of health information systems in the Philippines using the PRISMA systematic review framework, establishing that data fragmentation, paper-based workflow reliance, and lack of interoperable database architectures represent the primary operational barriers in local public healthcare facilities.

- **Relevance:**  
  The investigation provides rigorous empirical baseline data on the status of health informatics in Philippine primary care units and municipal health offices. Published in the *International Journal of Computing and Digital Systems*, the study categorizes structural challenges including duplicate patient data recording, manual queue delays, and non-standardized electronic reporting interfaces. However, the authors observe that existing local implementations often lack integrated queuing modules directly coupled to patient electronic medical records. These theoretical findings justify the architectural logic of the proposed Naga City Health Office system by validating the need for direct cross-module interoperability and centralized database storage. Specifically, the authors' identification of paper-based bottlenecks confirms that automating queue dispatch alongside instant record retrieval directly supports ISO/IEC 25010 performance efficiency objectives by eliminating redundant patient registration steps.
