# Medical data integration using HL7 standards for patient’s early identification

**Category:** [[Related Study International]]

**Adi A. AlQudah, Mostafa Al-Emran, and Khaled Shaalan. 2021. Medical data integration using HL7 standards for patient’s early identification. *PLOS ONE* 16, 12 (2021), e0262067. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0262067**

- **Summary:** To address operational bottlenecks and extended outpatient waiting intervals caused by manual intake procedures, this study evaluated an interoperability model integrating a Queue Management Solution (QMS) with Electronic Medical Records (EMR) across 517 valid appointments, demonstrating a reduction in total patient journey time by more than 14 minutes and patient identification time by 10 minutes.
- **Relevance:** The empirical findings justify the architectural design of the proposed Naga City Health Office system, as the documented 14-minute decrease in transit duration and 10-minute acceleration in record retrieval confirm that linking automated queuing directly to centralized electronic records eliminates registration bottlenecks and shortens triage delays.
- **Source Reference(s):**
  - Abstract & Section 5 (Results and Discussion), pp. 1–20.
  - Direct Quote: *"As a key finding, the proposed solution is efficient and could reduce the 'patient’s journey time' by more than 14 minutes and 'time to identify' patients by 10 minutes."* (Abstract, p. 1)
