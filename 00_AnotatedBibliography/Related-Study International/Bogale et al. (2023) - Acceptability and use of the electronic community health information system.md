# Acceptability and use of the electronic community health information system and its determinants among health extension workers in Ethiopia: a retrospective cross-sectional observational study

**Category:** [[Related Study International]]

**Tariku Nigatu Bogale, Herman Willems, Loko Abraham Bongassie, Yemariam Eyob, Chaluma Kumela Mengesha, Bantalem Yeshanew Yihun, Mesud Mohammed, Naod Wendrad, Gemechis Melkamu, Dawit Wolde Daka, Selamawit Meressa, and Tadesse Alemu Bekele. 2023. Acceptability and use of the electronic community health information system and its determinants among health extension workers in Ethiopia: a retrospective cross-sectional observational study. *BMC Medical Informatics and Decision Making* 23, 1 (2023), 290. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12911-023-02385-z**

- **Summary:** To identify key determinants governing the adoption of digital health platforms in public clinic settings, this cross-sectional observational study evaluated 587 healthcare workers utilizing structural equation modeling, establishing that perceived ease of use and operational usefulness directly determine the successful transition from paper records to electronic health information systems.
- **Relevance:** The empirical findings validate the front-end usability and component-based design choices of the proposed Naga City Health Office system, as the structural equation analysis proves that minimizing cognitive interface friction directly drives staff adoption and sustains daily data entry across municipal health information modules.
