# Artificial intelligence-assisted reduction in patients’ waiting time for outpatient process: a retrospective cohort study

**Category:** [[Related Study International]]

**Xiaoqing Li, Dan Tian, Weihua Li, Bin Dong, Hansong Wang, Jiajun Yuan, Biru Li, Lei Shi, Xulin Lin, Liebin Zhao, and Shijian Liu. 2021. Artificial intelligence-assisted reduction in patients’ waiting time for outpatient process: a retrospective cohort study. *BMC Health Services Research* 21, 1 (2021), 237. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-021-06248-z**

- **Summary:** To evaluate the operational impact of automated pre-consultation routing on clinic congestion, this retrospective cohort study analyzed 12,342 outpatient visits using propensity score matching, demonstrating that automated intake reduced median patient waiting time from 1.97 hours down to 0.38 hours.
- **Relevance:** The quantitative outcome provides empirical justification for the proposed Naga City Health Office system, as the statistically significant reduction in median waiting duration from 1.97 hours to 0.38 hours indicates that integrating automated intake and queue sequencing into digital intake workflows eliminates physical bottlenecks and accelerates daily patient throughput in high-volume municipal clinics.
- **Source Reference(s):**
  - Abstract & Section 3 (Results), pp. 1–8.
  - Direct Quote: *"The median waiting time was 0.38 (interquartile range: 0.20, 1.33) hours for the AI-assisted group compared with 1.97 (0.76, 3.48) hours for the conventional group (p < 0.05)."* (Abstract, p. 1)
