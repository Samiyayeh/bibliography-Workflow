## Annotated Analysis

**Summary:** To resolve severe waiting room congestion and uncoordinated service handoffs in outpatient clinics, this study developed and evaluated QKlinik, a web-based queue management platform built using Agile methodology that integrates QR-code-based digital ticket generation, live queue tracking, and pharmacy fulfillment, achieving an average usability score of 4.78 out of 5 across user evaluation trials.

**Relevance:** The architectural design of QKlinik validates the multi-station queue management structure of the proposed Naga City Health Office system, as its end-to-end integration between consultation queuing and dispensary fulfillment demonstrates that linking separate service counters within a unified web interface eliminates administrative bottlenecks between consecutive clinical stages. Specifically, QKlinik's high usability rating (4.78/5) supports the NCHO HIS user interface design, which similarly provides clear visual queue displays and real-time station transitions to keep municipal patients and healthcare workers informed without relying on manual intercoms or paper token distribution.
