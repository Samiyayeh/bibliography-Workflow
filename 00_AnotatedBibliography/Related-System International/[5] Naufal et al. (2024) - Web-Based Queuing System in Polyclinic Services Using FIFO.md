**Category:** [[Related System International]]

## ACM Citation

Mochammad Naufal, Yuli Astuti, and Fajar Akbar. 2024. Implementation Of a Web-Based Queuing System in Hospital Polyclinic Services Using the FIFO Method: Case Study of Karangpawitan Community Health Center. *Informatics Management, Engineering and Information System Journal* 1, 2 (2024), 112–118. https://doi.org/10.56447/imeisj.v1i2.246

## Annotated Analysis

**Summary:** Developed to resolve long wait times and unorganized physical queues in primary outpatient clinics, this study designed and implemented a web-based queuing system utilizing First-In, First-Out (FIFO) routing integrated with polyclinic registration, demonstrating improved patient scheduling order and efficient staff-to-patient allocation across clinical service counters.

**Relevance:** The empirical implementation reinforces the core queue sequencing logic of the proposed Naga City Health Office system, as applying structured First-In, First-Out dispatching to community health polyclinics proves that automating ticket assignment directly resolves hallway congestion and disorderly patient crowding. Specifically, the Karangpawitan health center deployment validates the NCHO HIS approach of binding each generated queue number to an active consultation ticket, ensuring fair, transparent patient progression across municipal health office stations.
