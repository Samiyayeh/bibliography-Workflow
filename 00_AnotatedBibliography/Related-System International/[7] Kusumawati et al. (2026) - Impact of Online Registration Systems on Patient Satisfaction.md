## Annotated Analysis

**Summary:** Synthesizing 14 peer-reviewed studies published between 2015 and 2025, this scoping review from Dr. Kariadi General Hospital found that online patient registration systems consistently reduced total outpatient waiting times by up to 75%, with all included studies reporting measurable improvements in patient satisfaction attributable to lower queue congestion, streamlined intake workflows, and reduced lobby crowding during peak clinic hours.

**Relevance:** The aggregated evidence across 14 implementations directly validates the operational rationale of the proposed Naga City Health Office system, as the consistent 75% ceiling on wait-time reduction demonstrates that replacing paper-based counter registration with digital intake is the highest-impact intervention available to public health facilities regardless of setting or resource level. Specifically, this review confirms that patient satisfaction improvements are driven not merely by speed, but by the visible, transparent reduction of physical crowding at registration desks — directly aligning with the NCHO HIS kiosk-and-display model that eliminates manual queuing at service counters.
