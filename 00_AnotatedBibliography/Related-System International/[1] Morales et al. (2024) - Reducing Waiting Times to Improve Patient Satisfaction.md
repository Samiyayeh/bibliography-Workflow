## ACM Citation

Jenny Morales, Fabián Silva-Aravena, and Paula Saez. 2024. Reducing Waiting Times to Improve Patient Satisfaction: A Hybrid Strategy for Decision Support Management. *Mathematics* 12, 23 (2024), 3743. https://doi.org/10.3390/math12233743

## Annotated Analysis

**Summary:** To address patient congestion across the registration, vitals, and doctor consultation stages of outpatient service delivery, this study developed a hybrid decision support system for hospital queue management that combines Multiple Linear Regression-based wait-time prediction with simulation modeling, demonstrating reductions of 15% in registration wait time, 20% in vitals wait time, and 25% in consultation wait time — cutting total per-patient system time by an average of 22.5 minutes.

**Relevance:** The empirical reductions documented across all three service stages directly validate the multi-station queue architecture of the proposed Naga City Health Office system, as the 15–25% wait-time improvements confirm that structuring outpatient flow through distinct, sequentially managed service points produces measurable efficiency gains at each clinical handoff. Specifically, the study's finding that the consultation stage yields the highest reduction (25%) when staff allocation is optimized supports the NCHO HIS design decision to treat consultation as the primary performance-sensitive node, where the system automatically surfaces the next patient's electronic health record on the physician's screen the moment a queue number is called — eliminating idle time between patient transitions.
