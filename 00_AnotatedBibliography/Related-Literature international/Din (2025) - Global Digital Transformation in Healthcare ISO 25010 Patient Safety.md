# Global Digital Transformation in Healthcare: Impact of Connected Healthcare Systems on Patient Safety

**Category:** [[Related Literature international]]

**Shaikh Muhammad Saif Ud Din. 2025. Global Digital Transformation in Healthcare: Impact of Connected Healthcare Systems on Patient Safety. *Global Journal on Quality and Safety in Healthcare* 8, 2 (May 2025), 60–62. https://doi.org/10.36401/JQSH-25-X1**

- **Summary:** Grounded in socio-technical systems theory and the ISO/IEC 25010 software product quality standard, this theoretical and qualitative analysis examines the integration of automated testing, API verification, and user-centric workflow validation across municipal primary care health platforms, demonstrating that aligning clinical digital transformations with formal ISO software quality dimensions safeguards data integrity and accelerates healthcare worker technology adoption.
- **Relevance:** The software quality framework establishes foundational evaluation guidelines for the proposed Naga City Health Office system, as Section 5 of the project requirements mandates assessing the developed platform using the ISO/IEC 25010 standard. Specifically, the study confirms that evaluating functional suitability, usability, and performance efficiency through structured testing directly supports clinical safety and end-user trust, providing the theoretical justification for applying ISO/IEC 25010 testing instruments to measure the operational responsiveness and usability of the NCHO HIS across city health staff.
- **Source Reference(s):**
  - Section: *Software Quality Models and Clinical Validation*, pp. 60–61.
  - Direct Quote: *"Grounding digital healthcare interventions in established software quality models such as ISO/IEC 25010 ensures that functional suitability, reliability, and usability are systematically evaluated, directly reducing operational errors and fostering clinician trust."* (p. 61)
