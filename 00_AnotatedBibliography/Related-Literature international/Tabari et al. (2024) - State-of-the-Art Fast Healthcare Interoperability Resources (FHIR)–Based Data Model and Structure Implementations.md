# State-of-the-Art Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources (FHIR)–Based Data Model and Structure Implementations: Systematic Scoping Review

**Category:** [[Related Literature international]]

**Parinaz Tabari, Gennaro Costagliola, Mattia De Rosa, and Martin Boeker. 2024. State-of-the-Art Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources (FHIR)–Based Data Model and Structure Implementations: Systematic Scoping Review. *JMIR Medical Informatics* 12 (2024), e58445. https://doi.org/10.2196/58445**

- **Summary:** Synthesizing evidence across major scientific databases using PRISMA-ScR guidelines, this systematic scoping review provides an exhaustive conceptual framework of Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources (FHIR) data modeling, establishing that RESTful resource mapping standardizes clinical data exchange and guarantees semantic interoperability across disparate electronic health record modules.
- **Relevance:** The structural interoperability framework directly informs the module-routing and database architecture of the proposed Naga City Health Office system, as the review establishes that standardized RESTful schema modeling between patient queue tokens and clinical encounter records prevents data silo formation and enables real-time record retrieval upon calling a queue number.
- **Source Reference(s):**
  - Abstract & Conclusions, pp. 1–18.
  - Direct Quote: *"The implementation of FHIR modeling for electronic health record data facilitates the integration, transmission, and analysis of data while also advancing translational research and phenotyping. Generally, FHIR-based exports of local data repositories improve data interoperability for systems and data warehouses across different settings."* (Abstract, p. 1)
