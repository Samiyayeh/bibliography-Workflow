# Security of Blockchain and AI-Empowered Smart Healthcare: Application-Based Analysis

**Category:** [[Related Literature international]]

**Abdulatif Alabdulatif, Ibrahim Khalil, and M. Saidur Rahman. 2022. Security of Blockchain and AI-Empowered Smart Healthcare: Application-Based Analysis. *Applied Sciences* 12, 21 (2022), 11039. https://doi.org/10.3390/app122111039**

- **Summary:** Analyzing threat vectors and security countermeasures across modern clinical health ecosystems, this review outlines cryptographic architectures, access control paradigms, and data protection protocols necessary to safeguard distributed electronic medical repositories against data interception and unauthorized tampering.
- **Relevance:** The security analysis directly justifies the cryptographic implementation requirements of the proposed Naga City Health Office system, as the literature establishes that integrating AES-256 data encryption and strict role-based access control is essential to ensure compliance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 and protect sensitive medical records against unauthorized access across municipal clinic endpoints.
- **Source Reference(s):**
  - Abstract & Section 2 (Threat Analysis), pp. 1–18.
  - Direct Quote: *"The paper provides a comprehensive review of the latest security challenges and their countermeasures within the smart healthcare environment... evaluating security and privacy threats to maintain data integrity and secure communication."* (Abstract, p. 1)
