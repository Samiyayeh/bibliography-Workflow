# Global strategy on digital health 2020–2025

**Category:** [[Related Literature international]]

**World Health Organization. 2021. *Global strategy on digital health 2020–2025*. World Health Organization, Geneva, Switzerland. https://iris.who.int/handle/10665/344249**

- **Summary:** The World Health Organization establishes an international policy framework outlining four global strategic objectives to strengthen public healthcare infrastructures through the institutional adoption of secure, interoperable electronic health records, standardized digital workflows, and person-centered health information architectures aligned with United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 3.
- **Relevance:** The policy framework provides international institutional context for the Naga City Health Office system by establishing that digital health interoperability, structured patient record standardization, and secure electronic data exchange are essential prerequisites for achieving United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 3 and modernizing municipal healthcare delivery.
- **Source Reference(s):**
  - Executive Summary & Strategic Objectives, pp. 1–56.
  - Direct Quote: *"The vision of the global strategy is to improve health for everyone, everywhere by accelerating the development and adoption of appropriate, accessible, affordable, scalable and sustainable person-centric digital health solutions..."* (Executive Summary, p. 12)
