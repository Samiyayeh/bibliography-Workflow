## Annotated Analysis

**Summary:** Developed for the City Health Centre of Caalibangbangan in Cabanatuan City, Philippines, this study created and evaluated "Metacare" — a cross-platform healthcare management system that automates patient health records, appointment scheduling for medical programs, and consultation tracking. Built using a progressive component-based UI framework and a relational MySQL backend, the system was evaluated by IT experts and community healthcare workers using the ISO/IEC 25010 standard, achieving highly acceptable ratings across functional suitability, reliability, and usability.

**Relevance:** The Metacare deployment directly aligns with the technical architecture and local government healthcare scope of the proposed Naga City Health Office system. Utilizing a modern component UI framework connected to a relational MySQL database in a Philippine city health center setting confirms that relational database management systems effectively handle clinical records and appointment schedules within local government health units. Furthermore, the system's ISO/IEC 25010 evaluation by both technical experts and local health personnel directly mirrors the quality assessment framework targeted for the NCHO HIS.
