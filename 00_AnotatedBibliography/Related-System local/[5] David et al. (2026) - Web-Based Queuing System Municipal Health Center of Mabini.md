## Annotated Analysis

**Summary:** Developed to resolve severe physical wait times, lobby overcrowding, and disorganized patient intake at the Municipal Health Center of Mabini in the Philippines, this study created and evaluated a web-based queuing system using a three-tier architectural structure and Rapid Application Development (RAD). The system features digital patient registration, automated queue ticket allocation, and real-time waiting status monitors, achieving an overall ISO-based software quality rating of 4.43 out of 5.00 ("Excellent") with high scores in operational efficiency, security, and user acceptability across clinic staff and patient evaluators.

**Relevance:** The Mabini health center deployment directly mirrors the operational setting and core problem space of the proposed Naga City Health Office system, confirming that replacing manual paper-based queue issuance with a web-based automated queuing engine eliminates physical congestion in Philippine local municipal health facilities. Specifically, the system's three-tier architecture and real-time status display demonstrate the technical feasibility and high user acceptability of deploying centralized queue displays and counter-routing within a local government healthcare unit, reinforcing the NCHO HIS queuing and counter-calling design.
