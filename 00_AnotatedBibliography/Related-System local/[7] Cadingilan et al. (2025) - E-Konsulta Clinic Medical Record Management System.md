## Annotated Analysis

**Summary:** Developed to eliminate the errors, delays, and file retrieval bottlenecks of manual paper-based clinic records in Pagadian City, Philippines, this study designed and evaluated "E-Konsulta" — a web-based clinic medical records management platform with integrated digital intake and follow-up capabilities. Software quality evaluation based on ISO standards demonstrated a 100% pass rate in core functional areas alongside high scores in user experience (98.57%), reliability (97.14%), and data security (95.71%).

**Relevance:** The E-Konsulta implementation directly validates the digitized medical record management module of the proposed Naga City Health Office system by demonstrating that transitioning community and institutional clinic workflows from paper records to a secure digital records database eliminates patient chart lookup delays and administrative backlogs in local Philippine health facilities. Furthermore, its high ratings in user experience (98.57%) and security (95.71%) reinforce the NCHO HIS objective of providing an intuitive, privacy-compliant EHR interface for health staff.
