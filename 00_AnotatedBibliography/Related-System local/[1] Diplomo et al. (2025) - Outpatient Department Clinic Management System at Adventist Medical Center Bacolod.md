## ACM Citation

Junic B. Diplomo, Jake R. Pomperada, and Dennis V. Madrigal. 2025. Outpatient Department Clinic Management System at Adventist Medical Center Bacolod. *Technium Social Sciences Journal* 72, 1 (2025), 185–196. https://doi.org/10.47577/tssj.v72i1.12884

## Annotated Analysis

**Summary:** Developed to eliminate manual paper record bottlenecks and long queues at Adventist Medical Center Bacolod in the Philippines, this hybrid outpatient clinic management system was built using Rapid Application Development to digitize appointment scheduling, patient intake, electronic records, and physician ordering, resulting in a 35% reduction in patient waiting times, a 50% improvement in record management efficiency, and a 92% user satisfaction rating among clinic personnel and patients.

**Relevance:** The empirical results directly support the implementation of the proposed Naga City Health Office system, as the 35% reduction in patient wait times and 50% improvement in record handling efficiency confirm that replacing manual logbooks with a centralized digital clinic workflow effectively relieves front-desk congestion in Philippine healthcare facilities. Specifically, the Bacolod implementation demonstrates that connecting patient registration and queue scheduling directly to electronic patient files produces high staff adoption (92% satisfaction), reinforcing the NCHO HIS design choice to automatically display a patient's medical records to attending health workers the moment their queue number is called.
