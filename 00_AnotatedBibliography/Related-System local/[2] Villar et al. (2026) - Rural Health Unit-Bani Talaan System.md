## Annotated Analysis

**Summary:** Developed to eliminate the operational delays and record loss caused by manual paper logbooks in a Philippine municipal health facility, this study created and deployed the RHU-Bani Talaan System, a centralized web-based patient recording platform that digitizes patient profiling, consultation history tracking, and reporting for Barangay Health Workers and clinical staff.

**Relevance:** The implementation outcomes directly validate the database architecture of the proposed Naga City Health Office system, as transitioning from paper logbooks to a centralized web repository demonstrated measurable acceleration in patient record retrieval and reduced administrative workloads for municipal healthcare workers. Specifically, the Bani RHU system confirms that organizing health records around unified patient identifiers enables seamless data retrieval during consultations, reinforcing the NCHO HIS mechanism of instantly querying and presenting a patient's historical medical file the moment their queue number is called.
