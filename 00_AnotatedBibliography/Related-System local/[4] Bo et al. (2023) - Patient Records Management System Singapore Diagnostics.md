## Annotated Analysis

**Summary:** Developed to address the surge in healthcare demand and patient intake volume across diagnostic partner facilities in the Philippines, this study implemented a web-based patient records and laboratory management system that automates demographic encoding, test order routing, and clinical data tracking. The system was rigorously evaluated against the ISO/IEC 25010 software product quality standard across functional suitability, usability, performance efficiency, and security, demonstrating substantial reductions in manual data entry errors and faster patient intake turnaround.

**Relevance:** The implementation directly informs the centralized EHR and intake module of the proposed Naga City Health Office system by demonstrating how web-based record management eliminates redundant data entry across distinct clinical touchpoints in Philippine healthcare settings. Furthermore, its formal evaluation under the ISO/IEC 25010 model — specifically verifying high ratings in performance efficiency, usability, and data security — provides direct methodological validation for evaluating the NCHO HIS software quality and operational responsiveness.
