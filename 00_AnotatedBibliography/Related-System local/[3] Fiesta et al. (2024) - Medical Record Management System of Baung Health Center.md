## ACM Citation

Clifford T. Fiesta, Tonette L. Lucas, Clarisa A. Dagdag, and Novelyn Grace Agustin. 2024. Medical Record Management System of Baung Health Center. *Journal of Pure and Applied Sciences* 2, 1 (April 2024). https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20726594

## Annotated Analysis

**Summary:** This study from Cagayan State University designed, developed, and deployed a digital Medical Record Management System for the Baung Health Center in Cagayan to replace its existing manual paper-based records process. Using Rapid Application Development (RAD), the system established a secure, role-controlled database with a user-friendly interface, backup redundancy, and printed record generation — streamlining operations, reducing human error, and improving patient data accessibility for authorized health center personnel.

**Relevance:** The paper directly parallels the EHR component of the proposed NCHO HIS by demonstrating that digitizing patient records at a Philippine community health center eliminates common bottlenecks of manual filing and retrieval. Specifically, the RAD-developed MRMS's architecture — where authorized personnel query a single secured database to retrieve historical health records — directly mirrors the proposed system's physician-facing module, which must instantly surface a patient's consolidated medical history upon calling a queue number, replacing fragmented paper file searches.
