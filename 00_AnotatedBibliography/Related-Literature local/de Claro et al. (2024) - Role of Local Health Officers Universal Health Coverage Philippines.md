# The role of local health officers in advancing public health and primary care integration: lessons from the ongoing Universal Health Coverage reforms in the Philippines

**Category:** [[Related Literature local]]

**Vergil de Claro, Juan Bernardo Lava, Clemencia Bondoc, and Laurentiu Stan. 2024. The role of local health officers in advancing public health and primary care integration: lessons from the ongoing Universal Health Coverage reforms in the Philippines. *BMJ Global Health* 9, 1 (January 2024), e014118. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjgh-2023-014118**

- **Summary:** Examining the implementation of Universal Health Care (UHC) reforms (RA 11223) across Philippine local government units, this health policy and systems study evaluates the strategic role of local health officers in overcoming decentralized healthcare fragmentation, demonstrating that integrating primary care delivery with digital health records and real-time coordination tools is essential for synchronizing clinic workflows and ensuring equitable patient access.
- **Relevance:** The policy framework for Philippine local health systems directly informs the institutional integration and deployment model of the proposed Naga City Health Office system, as RA 11223 mandates local government units to consolidate municipal clinic workflows into integrated primary care networks. Specifically, the study highlights that local health officers require centralized digital tools to eliminate siloed patient records and manage intake congestion across city health facilities, validating the NCHO HIS objective of providing unified queue tracking and centralized EHR management under municipal health administration.
