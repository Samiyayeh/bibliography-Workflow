# Formative Evaluation of the Implementation of eHealth in the Philippines: A Qualitative Study

**Category:** [[Related Literature local]]

**Bettina D. Evio and Sheila R. Bonito. 2024. Formative Evaluation of the Implementation of eHealth in the Philippines: A Qualitative Study. *Acta Medica Philippina* 58, 12 (2024), 35–47. https://doi.org/10.47895/amp.v58i12.9289**

- **Summary:** Synthesizing qualitative evaluations across 15 Philippine municipalities under the Philippine eHealth Strategic Framework and Plan, this review articulates a multidimensional implementation framework detailing how software complexity, interoperability, and local clinical workflow alignment dictate the operational sustainability of electronic medical record systems in public healthcare facilities.
- **Relevance:** The multidimensional evaluation framework validates the design and architectural choices of the proposed Naga City Health Office system, as the study demonstrates that minimizing software complexity, tailoring interfaces to municipal clinical workflows, and ensuring cross-module data interoperability are necessary to prevent administrative resource wastage and ensure high long-term adoption among public health staff.
- **Source Reference(s):**
  - Abstract & Discussion, pp. 35–47.
  - Direct Quote: *"Content-specific factors mainly include the eHealth design (complexity, adaptability to local context and service demands, interoperability or the capacity to connect or exchange information with other platforms/systems). Notably, limitations across the three dimensions could make eHealth implementation more complicated, which could lead to poor time management and resource wastage."* (Abstract, p. 35)
