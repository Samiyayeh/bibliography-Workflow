# Implementation of Outpatient Consultation Digitalization for Healthcare Services: Basis for Development Plan

**Category:** [[Related Literature local]]

**Emerson C. Angeles and Leonardo S. Garcia. 2024. Implementation of Outpatient Consultation Digitalization for Healthcare Services: Basis for Development Plan. *International Journal of Research and Innovation in Social Science (IJRISS)* 8, 12 (December 2024), 304–319. https://doi.org/10.47772/IJRISS.2024.8120304**

- **Summary:** Examining the transition from paper-based outpatient workflows to digital intake and consultation records across Philippine public healthcare facilities, this policy and implementation study identifies operational and legal bottlenecks — emphasizing that digital health system adoption requires structured compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) alongside dedicated staff training to mitigate privacy risks and administrative resistance.
- **Relevance:** The implementation and legal compliance framework directly informs the design and governance policies of the proposed Naga City Health Office system by establishing that digital queueing and health records platforms in Philippine public healthcare settings must embed strict data protection safeguards under RA 10173. Specifically, the study confirms that digitizing outpatient intake without robust role-based access control and patient consent protocols introduces compliance liabilities, validating the NCHO HIS requirement for role-restricted physician interfaces and encrypted database storage.
- **Source Reference(s):**
