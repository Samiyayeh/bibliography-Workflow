# Promises and Realities of Electronic Health Information System in the Philippines

**Category:** [[Related Literature local]]

**Arturo M. Ongkeko Jr. 2024. Promises and Realities of Electronic Health Information System in the Philippines. *Acta Medica Philippina* 58, 22 (2024), 1–4. https://doi.org/10.47895/amp.v58i22.12364**

- **Summary:** Analyzing the nationwide digital transformation of Philippine primary healthcare facilities, this review examines the operational bottlenecks of coexisting paper-based and digital records, presenting the Mind the GAPS (Governance, Architecture, Program Management, and Standards) framework to guide the standardization and interoperable architecture of electronic health information systems under the Universal Health Care Law.
- **Relevance:** The analysis directly substantiates the architectural requirements of the proposed Naga City Health Office system, as the review demonstrates that eliminating dual paper-digital workflows through standardized electronic records and unified interoperability blueprints is essential to achieve sustainable system adoption and fulfill the service delivery objectives of the Philippine Universal Health Care Law.
- **Source Reference(s):**
  - Abstract & Main Text, pp. 1–4.
  - Direct Quote: *"The adoption of electronic HIS reflects a complex interplay of full digital transition, systemic barriers, and user engagement challenges... What Macabasag et al. describe as an 'ambivalent transition' characterizes the shift from paper-based to electronic systems, where both coexist due to resource constraints... The Asia eHealth Information Network (AeHIN) introduced the Mind the GAPS (Governance, Architecture, Program Management, and Standards) Framework as key pillars for building integrated and interoperable systems."* (Abstract, p. 1)
