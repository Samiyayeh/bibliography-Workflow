# Determinants of Telemedicine Acceptance among Doctors-to-the-Barrios (DTTBs) in the Philippines

**Category:** [[Related Literature local]]

**Edgardo Miguel V. Austria, Carl Froilan D. Leochico, Sharon D. Ignacio, and Jose Alvin P. Mojica. 2024. Determinants of Telemedicine Acceptance among Doctors-to-the-Barrios (DTTBs) in the Philippines. *Acta Medica Philippina* 58, 20 (November 2024), 9–19. https://doi.org/10.47895/amp.v58i20.9403**

- **Summary:** Investigating technology adoption factors across 116 rural community physicians in the Philippine Department of Health's Doctors-to-the-Barrios program, this cross-sectional study models the organizational, behavioral, and policy determinants of digital healthcare acceptance, demonstrating that practitioner self-efficacy and institutional culture are primary drivers of clinical health technology adoption in decentralized Philippine public health facilities.
- **Relevance:** The technology adoption model establishes critical user-experience and deployment guidelines for the proposed Naga City Health Office system by identifying the human and institutional factors that govern physician acceptance of digital healthcare systems in Philippine municipal health units. Specifically, the study demonstrates that digital systems must provide intuitive, low-complexity interfaces that directly support clinical decision-making without introducing workflow overhead, validating the NCHO HIS requirement for clean physician-facing queue calling and automated EHR retrieval modules.
